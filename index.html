About Vellore Institute of Engineering & Technology

Founded in 2002 by the Sri Balaji Educational Trust, VIET started with three undergraduate engineering branches and 180 students. Today, it stands as a premier autonomous institution spread across a green 65-acre campus.

Our academic framework emphasizes outcome-based education, continuous internal assessment, and mandatory project-based learning from the second year onwards.

Our Vision

To emerge as a centre of excellence in technical education and applied research, nurturing competent and socially responsible engineers.

Our Mission

To impart quality engineering education through modern laboratories, industry collaboration, ethical values, and lifelong learning.
